Privacy notice

Last updated October 2026.

What we collect

Your name, email and optional phone number; the details of each case you open; and the documents and messages you upload as evidence. Evidence can include sensitive information such as financial records and identity documents. Please do not upload medical records.

Why we use it

Only to let you organise, document and pursue your own complaints and claims: storing your evidence, building your timeline and letters, and reminding you to follow up. The legal bases are your explicit consent, given when you create an account, and the establishment and exercise of your own legal claims (Data Protection Act 2019, section 45).

Who can see it

Only you. Uploaded files are encrypted at rest and can only be opened through your signed-in account. We do not sell your data or share it with the organisations you complain about. We never send anything to anyone on your behalf: you send your own letters.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account is open. Cases closed for more than 24 months are deleted automatically, with their documents. You can delete a case or your whole account at any time.

Your rights

You can download all your data from Account → Download my data, correct your details, and permanently delete your account and documents from the account page. You can also withdraw consent by deleting your account. If you have a concern, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (odpc.go.ke).

Security incidents

If a breach affects your data we will notify the Data Protection Commissioner within 72 hours and tell you where the law requires.

Contact

Questions about your data: write to the address shown in the footer of our emails.
